# 0003: Portal cookies and scoped device sessions

Context: sellers, admins, anonymous kiosk shoppers and OTP customers have different lifetimes.
Decision: JWT access tokens last 15 minutes for portal and 10 minutes for customers.
Portal uses HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict cookies; unsafe cookie requests check Origin.
Refresh tokens are random, hashed as Valkey keys, single-use and expire after 7 days.
Bearer validation also checks the current database user status and tenant.
Kiosk keys are SHA-256 matched to one enabled device.
OTP is HMAC-protected in Valkey for 3 minutes, with 5 failures locking for 15 minutes.
OTP issuance has a 60-second cooldown; customer sessions have no refresh.
Production SMS requires a configured HTTPS delivery adapter; missing delivery fails closed.
Consequence: development simulation must be explicitly enabled, never silently used in production.
